## 5. Outliers and Data Quality Issues

### Missing area-year combinations: None — complete coverage.


### YoY anomalies (|change| > 60% or change < -30%): 0 rows

None detected in terms of extreme single-year swings.

**Notable: Killarney volatility**
Killarney shows unusual multi-year swings that are worth flagging: -17.2% in 2013,
-8.7% in 2016, -13.0% in 2020. These are the most volatile patterns in the panel.
The likely cause: Killarney's neighbourhood codes (018, 023, 024, 025) are LOW-to-MEDIUM
confidence multi-area splits, meaning a meaningful share of Killarney's properties are
attributed to it from codes that are primarily mapped to other areas (Sunset, Marpole,
Renfrew-Collingwood). Assessment boundary changes or composition shifts between years
could cause these swings. Treat Killarney figures with extra caution in Phase 4.

## 6. Methodology Notes


**Neighbourhood code mapping:**
The property tax files identify properties with a numeric `neighbourhood_code` (001–030),
not directly by local area name. The lookup table (`neighbourhood_code_lookup.csv`) maps
each code to one or two local areas with confidence weights derived from geocoding.

- **Median/mean values**: computed using weighted assignment. Each property is included
  in every area mapped to its neighbourhood code, but contributes a weight proportional
  to that area's share in the lookup table. A weighted median and weighted mean are then
  computed per area-year. This ensures secondary areas (Dunbar-Southlands, Fairview,
  Shaughnessy, Strathcona) are represented even though no code has them as its primary.
- **Total land value and property count**: distributed fractionally across areas using
  the lookup weights. A property in a 60/40 split code contributes 60% to area A and
  40% to area B.

**Coverage gap — 6 of 22 CoV local areas have no neighbourhood code mapping:**
The BCA neighbourhood codes 001–030 do not cover: Grandview-Woodland, Kensington-Cedar
Cottage, Kitsilano, South Cambie, Victoria-Fraserview, West Point Grey. These areas
produce no rows in this panel. This is a source data limitation — the property records
for those areas may use different codes or none at all. For Phase 4, the join to the
business panel will need a fallback strategy for businesses located in these 6 areas.

**YoY change:** computed from consecutive annual medians in this panel, NOT from the
`previous_land_value` column in the source data (unavailable pre-2013, and represents
previous assessment year not necessarily previous calendar year).

**Cumulative growth:** indexed to 2007 (first full data year). Areas
missing 2007 data will show NaN cumulative growth.

**CURRENT_LAND_VALUE** is the assessed market value per BC Assessment. Excludes
improvement value. Taxable value differs due to exemptions and mill rate adjustments.

**Tax levy** is excluded from this analysis (blank until June each year per documentation).
